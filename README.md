# 📊 Madhav E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 👨‍💻 Author

**Satyam Singh**

**Project:** Madhav E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, quantity, customer behavior, product categories, payment methods, and geographical performance.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Madhav E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** provides an interactive view of e-commerce business performance through dynamic KPIs, charts, filters, and visual analytics.

The dashboard is designed to help business users quickly understand:

* Overall sales and profit performance
* Product and category contribution
* State-wise sales performance
* Monthly profit trends
* Customer-wise sales contribution
* Payment method distribution
* Sub-category profitability
* Quarterly performance

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs
* **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts and dashboard design

---

## 📌 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard highlights key business performance indicators including:

| KPI                           | Description                     |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Total Profit**              | Overall profit generated        |
| **Total Amount**              | Total sales/order amount        |
| **Total Quantity**            | Total quantity of products sold |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Average value of orders         |

The dashboard also provides **Quarter** and **State** filters for interactive analysis.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. 💰 Profit by Month

A monthly profit analysis is used to identify:

* High and low profit months
* Monthly fluctuations in profitability
* Periods of positive and negative profit performance
* Overall profit trends throughout the year

### 2. 🗺️ Amount by State

The state-wise analysis helps identify geographical sales performance and highlights the states contributing the highest sales amount.

### 3. 📦 Quantity by Category

Category-level analysis shows the contribution of different product categories to total quantity sold.

This helps understand which categories have stronger demand.

### 4. 👤 Amount by Customer

Customer-level analysis highlights the contribution of major customers to overall sales.

This can help identify high-value customers and understand customer purchasing contribution.

### 5. 💳 Quantity by Payment Mode

The dashboard analyzes product quantity across different payment methods, helping understand customer payment preferences.

### 6. 🛍️ Profit by Sub-Category

Sub-category profitability analysis helps identify which products contribute most significantly to overall profit.

This can support product-level and inventory-related business decisions.
